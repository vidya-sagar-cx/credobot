Customers requesting account closure at X Corp Bank must submit a formal written or verified digital application accompanied by valid identification. The designated account must carry a strictly zero balance with all pending overdraft charges, negative balances, and service dues cleared prior to final processing. If the account is closed within one calendar year of opening, a standard early closure fee of ₹500 is debited to offset administrative expenses. Applicants must surrender all unused cheque-book leaves, active debit cards, and banking instruments to the branch for secure disposal before closure certificates are generated.
