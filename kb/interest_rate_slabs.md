X Corp Bank prices loan products through standardized interest-rate slabs linked to the borrower's external credit score and loan category. Personal Loans range from 10.50 percent per annum for credit scores of 750 and above, increasing to 13.00 percent for scores between 700 and 749, and 16.50 percent for scores below 700. Home Loans offer preferential base rates starting at 8.40 percent for borrowers with scores of 750 or higher, moving up to 9.25 percent for intermediate credit bands. Auto Loans carry interest rates between 8.75 percent and 11.50 percent, while specialized Business Loans start at 11.00 percent and scale upward based on annual balance sheet risk ratings.
