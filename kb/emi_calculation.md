Equated Monthly Installments at CRED are calculated using the reducing-balance amortization formula where EMI equals P multiplied by r multiplied by (1 plus r) to the power n divided by ((1 plus r) to the power n minus 1). In this computation, P represents the principal amount borrowed, r denotes the monthly interest rate derived by dividing the annual rate by 12, and n reflects the total loan tenure in months. The calculated installment amount is rounded off to the nearest whole rupee to standardize billing schedules across customer accounts. The borrower's first EMI payment date is scheduled exactly one calendar month following loan disbursal or on the immediate designated billing cycle date.
