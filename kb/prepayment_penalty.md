Individual borrowers holding floating-rate retail loans with CRED are subject to nil prepayment penalties when making partial or full principal prepayments. Fixed-rate retail loans entail a prepayment fee of 2 percent on the prepaid principal sum if the repayment occurs after the mandatory six-month initial lock-in period. Borrowers may not execute partial prepayment during the first six months from disbursal to preserve initial loan underwriting costs. Commercial and corporate business term loans remain exempt from the retail zero-penalty waiver and attract a contractual early termination charge of up to 3 percent of the outstanding facility balance.
