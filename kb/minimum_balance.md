X Corp Bank mandates that savings account holders maintain a specified average monthly balance determined by the geographic location of the home branch. Branch requirements specify an average monthly balance of ₹10,000 in metropolitan centers, ₹5,000 in urban branches, and ₹2,500 in semi-urban or rural branch locations. Failure to maintain the stipulated balance triggers a proportional shortfall penalty capped at a maximum of ₹400 plus applicable statutory taxes per calendar month. Basic Savings Bank Deposit accounts, student accounts, and corporate salary accounts are completely exempt from minimum balance requirements.
