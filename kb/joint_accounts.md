CRED allows up to four individuals to open and hold a joint deposit or loan account under shared financial responsibility. Operating mandates can be configured as Either or Survivor where any individual holder may execute transactions, or Jointly where all account holders must sign every transaction. Every joint account requires a designated nominee who is entitled to receive account proceeds in the unfortunate event of the demise of all primary account holders. Minor individuals aged ten years and older may be added as joint holders with a legal natural guardian overseeing operational limits until legal majority.
