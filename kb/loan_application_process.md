The loan application lifecycle at X Corp Bank transitions sequentially across five formal operational statuses comprising Submitted, Under Review, Approved, Rejected, and Disbursed. An application logged as Submitted enters initial document intake, moving to Under Review within two to four business days as lending officers examine borrower credentials. If underwriting algorithms or underwriters detect irregular documentation or risk signals, the file is flagged for fraud review and held pending compliance inspection. Clear applications progress to Approved and reach Disbursed within 48 hours, while complex cases require managerial escalation to the lending operations team for manual sign-off.
