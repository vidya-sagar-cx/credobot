An individual's credit score is calculated using five major financial factors with timely payment history holding the highest relative weight. Maintaining revolving credit utilization below 30 percent of total authorized credit limits positively influences the composite score by demonstrating responsible credit management. A prolonged credit history with well-seasoned loan facilities improves creditworthiness over short-lived borrowing profiles. Frequent hard credit inquiries initiated by lenders within brief time intervals depress scores, whereas maintaining a healthy balance between secured home loans and unsecured credit lines strengthens the profile.
