X Corp Bank credit cards feature a standard joining and annual renewal fee of ₹1,500 that is automatically waived if the cardholder spends at least ₹1,00,000 during the preceding calendar year. Late-payment charges are assessed according to outstanding statement balance slabs, ranging from ₹200 for modest balances up to ₹1,000 for balances exceeding ₹50,000. Cash advances transacted at automated teller machines attract a flat withdrawal transaction fee of 2.5 percent of the advanced cash or a minimum of ₹500. All international card transactions settled in foreign currencies incur a foreign-exchange currency markup fee of 3.5 percent on the gross converted amount.
