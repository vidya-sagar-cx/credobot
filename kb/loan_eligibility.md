CRED evaluates loan eligibility based on borrower age bands, net monthly income, credit score thresholds, and employment vintage across retail lending categories. Salaried applicants for Personal Loans and Auto Loans must be between 21 and 58 years of age with a minimum net monthly salary of ₹25,000 and at least one year of continuous work experience. Home Loan borrowers require a minimum household income of ₹50,000 per month, an age limit extending up to 65 years at loan maturity, and a minimum credit score of 720. Education Loan eligibility demands admission to an accredited university program with a creditworthy co-applicant, whereas MSME Business Loans require at least three years of audited business operations and a minimum annual turnover of ₹30,00,000.
