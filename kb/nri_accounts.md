Non-Resident Indians and Persons of Indian Origin meeting FEMA residency definitions are eligible to maintain offshore accounts with X Corp Bank. Eligible customers may establish Non-Resident External accounts in Indian Rupees, Non-Resident Ordinary accounts for managing domestic Indian income, or Foreign Currency Non-Resident deposits in major global currencies. Funds maintained within NRE and FCNR accounts remain fully and freely repatriable overseas without regulatory limits or exchange restrictions. Interest income earned on NRE and FCNR deposits is completely exempt from Indian income tax, whereas interest accrued in NRO accounts is subject to domestic tax deducted at source.
