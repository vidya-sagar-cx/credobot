Every retail banking customer opening an account or securing credit with X Corp Bank must submit a valid Permanent Account Number or an authorized declaration. Customers must also provide one officially valid document as verified proof of identity and current residential address, such as an Aadhaar card, passport, voter identity card, or driving licence. Current residential address verification can additionally be substantiated with a recent utility bill or municipal tax receipt dated within the prior three months. Under regulatory compliance standards, re-KYC profile reviews are mandatorily scheduled every two years for high-risk customers, every eight years for medium-risk customers, and every ten years for low-risk customers.
